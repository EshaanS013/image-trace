# Limitations

- Metadata can be absent, incorrect, rewritten by normal software, or stripped during transfer.
- SHA-256 equality establishes byte identity with the recorded acquired copy; it does not prove the truth, date, place, authorship, or authenticity of image content.
- An editing-software tag only establishes that the tag value is present.
- Camera grouping uses reported make/model metadata and is not device-level source attribution.
- Coordinates can be stale or inaccurate. Routes and speeds are derived from selected values, not observations of physical travel.
- Timezone-naive timestamps cannot be safely compared across places without a documented analyst assumption.
- HEIC support depends on a safely installed parser and is not enabled in the default MVP build.
- The local in-process job runner is appropriate for the MVP but not a multi-user distributed deployment.
- IMAGE TRACE is not a court-certified forensic suite and does not provide legal conclusions.
